Our Story

More than a café.
A feeling.

Tucked in the heart of Aurangpura, Suto Café was born from a simple idea — that every person deserves a place to pause. Whether you're catching up with friends, stealing a quiet hour alone, or sharing a meal with someone special, Suto wraps you in warmth.

Our menu blends global café favourites with locally loved flavours, prepared with high-quality ingredients and served with genuine care. As the wall says — "More Espresso, Less Depresso."
